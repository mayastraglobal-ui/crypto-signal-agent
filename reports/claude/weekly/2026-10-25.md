## SMC/ICT concept
**The concept: kill zones and the Silver Bullet.** The ICT claim is that price is "delivered" in fixed daily windows, so the same setup works better inside them. S7-SILVER-BULLET uses this.

**Evidence (critical view):**
1. Source 1 finds real intraday patterns in Bitcoin. That supports the idea that hours differ.
2. But it says the pattern changes with big jumps, FOMC and liquidity. A fixed clock window ignores that.
3. The abstract does not give hours, so it neither confirms nor rejects the ICT windows.
4. Our numbers cannot judge it: S7-SILVER-BULLET-5M 15m is "too few trades vs its twin".

**Why retail traders fail with it:** the window is learned from charts after the fact, a window with few trades hides a small sample, and on 5m the fees take a large share of R.

**What to do:** nothing by eye. The card stays in testing against its twin. Record: `memory/smc_research.md`.

## How traders lose money
**Case study: day trading in Brazil (source 2).**
1. Everyone who started day trading Brazilian equity index futures in 2013-2015 and lasted at least 300 days was followed.
2. 97% lost money. Only 0.4% earned more than a bank teller (US$54 a day).
3. The best trader made about US$310 a day with a standard deviation of US$2,560.
4. There was no sign of learning over time. The authors say individuals can barely compete with high-frequency traders.
