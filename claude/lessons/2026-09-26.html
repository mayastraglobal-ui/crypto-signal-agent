Beginner lesson 2 of 14: The stop-loss comes first

The stop-loss is the price where the idea is wrong and the trade closes with a small loss. Every signal here has its stop decided BEFORE entry; it never moves further away. Without a stop, one bad trade can wipe out many good ones. Try it: an [ENTRY] email shows entry, stop and targets - the stop is always there.
