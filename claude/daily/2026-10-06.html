Curriculum

  • Today's item: C03, "Common Risk Factors in Cryptocurrency", Yukun Liu, Aleh Tsyvinski, Xi Wu. The NBER page lists the published version as The Journal of Finance 77(2), 2022, pages 1133-1177. The plan's reference matches.
  • Opened: https://www.nber.org/papers/w25882 and the working paper PDF (May 2019). I read the working paper, not the final journal text.
  • What it says, in simple words:
  • Each week, coins are sorted into five groups by a characteristic and held for one week.
  • Three things explain which coins do better: the whole coin market, size (small coins did better) and momentum (coins that rose more kept rising more).
  • Momentum over 1, 2, 3 and 4 weeks worked: the top group beat the bottom group by 2.7%, 3.3%, 4.1% and 2.5% per week. Momentum over 8 weeks or longer did not.
  • Momentum worked among the larger coins (4.2% per week) but not among the smaller ones (0.6%, not significant).
  • Data: 2014 to 2018, coins above $1 million, before trading costs.
  • What it means for us:
  • Our coins are all large coins, the group where momentum showed up, and the horizon (weeks) fits our 4h cells better than 30m or 1h.
  • This is a relative idea (stronger than other coins), and our cards only look at one coin, plus BTC through btc_ret.
  • It is not evidence that our cards work: old data, thousands of small coins, and no costs.
