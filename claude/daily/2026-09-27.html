Curriculum

  • Today's item: C01, "Time series momentum", Tobias J. Moskowitz, Yao Hua Ooi, Lasse Heje Pedersen, Journal of Financial Economics 104 (2012) 228-250. The reference in the plan is correct.
  • Opened: the authors' PDF (https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~lpederse/papers/TimeSeriesMomentum.pdf) and AQR's summary page (https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Journal-Article/Time-Series-Momentum).
  • What it says, in simple words:
  • In all 58 futures they tested (stock indexes, currencies, commodities, bonds; 1985 to 2009), a market's own return over the last 12 months predicted its next month.
  • The trend lasts about 1 to 12 months, then partly reverses.
  • Their main rule: look back 12 months, hold 1 month.
  • It lost sharply when the 2008 crisis ended (March to May 2009). A sudden turn is how trend following loses.
  • The results I read are before costs. I found no cost-adjusted result in the text.
  • What it means for us: it supports "slow trends persist". That fits our only positive cell being the 4h breakout, not 1h or 30m. But the paper works in months and in futures, not in hours and crypto. It is a reason to test an idea, not proof that our strategy works.
