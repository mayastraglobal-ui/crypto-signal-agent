  • What the reviews say, in simple words:
  • The book sorts traders into groups: informed traders, news traders, dealers (who supply liquidity), front-runners, bluffers, and "utilitarian" traders (investors, borrowers, hedgers) who trade for reasons other than profit.
  • Trading is mostly a zero-sum game. Profit-seeking traders can only win, in total, from people who trade for other reasons.
  • The two reviewers disagree. One says chart and trend traders are mostly the losers; the other says trend followers win from hedgers and emotional traders. Both have a stake in their view.
  • What it means for us:
  • Every card's edge block names "who pays".

  • Our failed mean-reversion cards named "panic sellers", who are just other speculators.
